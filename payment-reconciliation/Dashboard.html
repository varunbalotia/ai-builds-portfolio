Reconciliation Overview

This dashboard analyzes uploaded transaction data to automatically detect and classify reconciliation discrepancies. The system maps records between the internal Platform and external Bank files, identifying Timing Gaps (month mismatches), Rounding Errors (sum mismatches), Duplicates (1:N mappings), and Orphans (unmatched records or standalone refunds). Use the interface below to explore identified issues and download the finalized, corrected ledger.
